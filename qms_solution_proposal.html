Rollout Waves

Wave 1: Core workflows Wave 2: Integrations + data model Wave 3: AI controls Wave 4: Predictive operations
  • Wave 1 (8-12 weeks): digitize deviation, CAPA, and change workflows.
  • Wave 2 (6-10 weeks): connect ERP/MES/LIMS and enforce canonical quality model.
  • Wave 3 (6-8 weeks): deploy AI triage and risk scoring with approval controls.
  • Wave 4 (4-6 weeks): scale real-time risk cockpit and closed-loop automation.

Patient Safety Guardrails

  • AI remains decision support only; no autonomous closure for patient-impacting records.
  • High-risk records require documented human review and accountable sign-off.
  • Control failures trigger release hold and mandatory escalation.
  • Decision lineage is retained end-to-end for inspections and investigations.
